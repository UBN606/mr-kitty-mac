MR. KITTY FOR YOUR MAC

INSTALL
1. Double-click the ZIP and drag Mr Kitty.app into Applications.
2. Open Mr Kitty from Applications. If macOS blocks it, follow Apple's
   Open Anyway directions: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445
3. If macOS asks about Accessibility, allow Mr Kitty. This lets his chat
   buttons open ChatGPT or Claude's small chat window.
4. If you already have Mr. Kitty in CoPet, hide that copy so you see one cat.

USE
- Drag Kitty to move him. Right-click Kitty to quit.
- Pencil: open a small text chat with the selected app.
- Microphone: open voice input. With ChatGPT, tap Voice in the Chat Bar.
- Star or Option-click Kitty: he rolls over, then gets a treat.
- G/C: switch between ChatGPT and Claude. Both apps must be installed to use
  their respective chat buttons. ChatGPT and Claude keep their own chats.

For Claude, enable Quick Entry in Claude Desktop > Settings > General >
Desktop App. Its voice shortcut uses Caps Lock on macOS 14 or later.

If anything is confusing, copy this into ChatGPT or Claude:

"Please help me install or use my Mr. Kitty Mac desktop pet, one step at a
time. Ask what I see and for the exact error or a screenshot. Use the steps
above and official Apple, ChatGPT, or Claude guidance. Explain any Terminal
